Interactive Media Assignment 1

Chosen Web Project: https://www.myoungaekim.com/

Question 1: What was the first thing you paid attention to when interacting with the experience?

The first thing I paid attention to was the collection of boxes on the left of the screen featuring the artist’s information.

Question 2: Spend two minutes with the experience and create a list of each of your discrete actions.

  • I first started scrolling to see how far I could scroll and how much information was available
  • I clicked around on certain hyperlinks to see where it would lead me
  • I interacted with the boxes on the left of the home page that revealed the advent calendar feature of the site
  • I moved around the advent calendar icons to see if anything occurred

Question 3: What part of the experience did you spend the most time engaging with?

The advent calendar easter egg/feature.

Question 4: What was the most common action in your two minute interaction with the experience?

The most common action was scrolling.

Question 5: What is your impression of the intended primary goal of the interactive experience?

To show the artist’s skills with motion graphics and web design and to also maintain user attention.

Question 6: How does the interactive experience communicate this primary goal?

It shows the designer’s capabilities in a small digestible dose which is simple enough for the user to interact with and understand while also planting information in the midst of the interaction about the designer.

Question 7: What is your impression of how the experience should be interacted with over time? (For how long and how many different times)

To me, the experience seems novel only the first couple of times, due to its simplicity. I believe the intention was just to have users engage with it for only a short amount of time around thirty seconds to a couple minutes at most.

Question 8:How does the interactive experience communicate how it should be interacted with over time?

As mentioned, the simplicity of it and limited amount of actions available show the user that it’s not meant to be engaged with over a certain amount of time.

Question 9:What other media forms (digital or otherwise) does the experience reference?

It explicitly references the advent calendar.

Question 10: What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should act when engaging with your research experience?

Considering that it is a recreation of an advent calendar, I felt that I should be in for some surprises or treats whenever I click on a different box.

Question 11: What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should feel when engaging with your research experience?

I felt as though it offers some lightheartedness and playfulness that also represents the designer’s spirit.

Question 12: What is the most frustrating part of the interaction to you and what makes that part frustrating?

Some of the boxes were not clickable and it sort of spoiled the flow of the interaction which made it frustrating.

Question 13: What is the most satisfying part of the interaction to you and what makes that part satisfying?

The smoothness of the animations and the motion graphics makes it feel easy and clean which makes it feel satisfying.
